About Indra

Indra is a student-driven club at CUSAT dedicated to advancing robotics, automation, artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL).

We foster innovation and technical expertise through workshops, lectures on emerging technologies and hands-on projects. Our initiatives include taking part in national and international competitions such as Robo Wars, and developing cutting-edge projects in robotics and AI.

We are committed to a collaborative environment that empowers students to explore and excel in automation and intelligent systems.

  • RoboticsDesign, build and drive robots for competitions and research.
  • AutomationSensors, controllers and systems that run without a human in the loop.
  • AI and MLModels that learn from data, from first principles to deployed projects.
  • Deep learningNeural networks for vision, language and control.
